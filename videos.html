Explaining the research in motion: films and talks on forecasting air pollution with physics and learning.

Foehn from A to Z, an animated series

Twelve animated episodes and a full film of about 44 minutes explain how Foehn works, block by block: the problem, the grids, the data, FLEXGREEN, the Green’s function and the tracers, the generator, flow matching, training in three stages, the global forecast, the results and the conclusion. The numbers are those of the preprint. English, with AI-dubbed audio on the episodes (up to 10 languages: French, Chinese, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Russian, Japanese, Arabic, Hindi and Finnish), translated subtitles and titles. The narration is generated by AI from my own voice, and the music is generated by AI.

Playlist on YouTube YouTube channel Foehn arXiv

Foehn: forecasting air pollution at 1 km, everywhere on Earth

An eight-minute film on Foehn, our global kilometre-scale probabilistic forecast of PM2.5: from the particles you breathe and the people whom coarse grids hide, to the air followed backwards through the weather forecast (FLEXGREEN), the generator that turns noise into a forecast, and how well it works at thousands of stations. Real data throughout; subtitles available.
