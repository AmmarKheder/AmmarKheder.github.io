2026

October 2026

  • #PROJECT 23 Oct Released Foehn from A to Z, an animated series that explains how Foehn works: twelve episodes and a full film of about 44 minutes, covering the problem, the grids, the data, FLEXGREEN, the Green’s function and the tracers, the generator, flow matching, training, the global forecast and the results. English, with AI-dubbed audio on the episodes (up to 10 languages) and translated subtitles; narration and music are generated by AI. Watch the series or the playlist on YouTube.
  • #PUB 19 Oct Preprint of Foehn, Global probabilistic forecasting of air pollution (with M. Boy and Z.-S. Liu), on arXiv: kilometre-scale, calibrated ensembles of PM2.5 up to a week ahead over all inhabited land. Interactive forecasts, an 8-minute film and the code at ammarkheder.com/Foehn.

September 2026

  • #TALK 7 Sep Presented TopoFlow, our physics-guided Vision Transformer for high-resolution air quality prediction, at IAC 2026 (International Aerosol Conference) in Xi’an, China. Great conversations with the community on day one.

Hosted by Dongjie Shang.
